## Typography and spacing

- Inter or system sans for website text. H1 36-56px responsive, heavy but not compressed. Body 16-18px, ~1.6 line-height.
- Use Press Start 2P only for small licensed decorative labels, not normal website body copy.
- 8px spacing system; 16/24/32px for close-to-distant relationships; 12px cards; 8px buttons; touch targets >=44px.
- Icon exports: 256px standard and optional 512px high-density. Use CSS pixelated scaling for true low-res pixel art, preserve transparency and original proportions.
- Use genuine property photographs only when advertising an actual property with rights and verified facts. Illustrated neighborhoods are editorial brand art, not official neighborhood maps.

## Layout language

A slim gold brand rule; white header with original logo; navy hero with large accessible headline; gold conversion CTA with navy text; three-step cards; a few illustrated Philly neighborhood cards; white-and-blue content panels; dark footer. The website can be richer than the companion email, but maintain recognizable cross-channel identity.

## Editorial and legal safeguards

Language should be local, informed, clear, and non-pushy. Avoid fake testimonials, unverified cash-offer guarantees, fabricated business tenure, license assertions or closing timeframes. Do not use SEPTA or municipal seals, stadium/sports team trademarks, or official-map representations without legal review. Real-estate advertising identity/licensing requirements and actual mailing address require owner verification. Keep contact-form consent separate from marketing email opt-in. Maintain accessible contrast, keyboard navigation, alt text and plain HTML headlines.
